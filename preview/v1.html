Built for the ten seconds you actually spend in a remote.

The official app keeps adding screens. Somos InControl does the opposite: the controls you reach for, and nothing else.

Instant volume

A big slider pinned at the top of every room, − / + buttons, mute, group volume, and a per-room max volume limit you set by holding a row.

Real EQ controls

Bass, treble, loudness and Trueplay in every room. Balance on stereo pairs; surround, height, subwoofer and crossover wherever the hardware exists.

Sound presets

Three TV and three music presets per room. Hold to save the room's EQ — and, if you tick them, volume, Night Sound, Speech Boost and Loudness. Tap to bring it all back.

Home theater, in one place

TV / Music source, audio-format badge, Night Sound, Speech Boost, height, surround, Sub and Group Audio Delay — shown only in soundbar rooms.

Lock Screen, widget and Siri

A Live Activity with volume, mute, Night Sound and music transport; a Home Screen widget with presets and volume; Siri phrases that work out of the box.

Every room, grouped or not

Home theaters, stereo pairs, singles and portables. Group rooms from any page, pin four rooms to the bottom bar, save the groups you use most.

Every feature, documented.

The user guide at docs.somosincontrol.com covers first launch, permissions, every control by room type, troubleshooting and the privacy policy — kept up to date with each beta build.
