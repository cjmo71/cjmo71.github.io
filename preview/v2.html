Real screenshots from the current TestFlight build. Nothing staged.

The official app keeps adding screens. This one keeps taking them away.

1

Volume is always first

A large slider pinned at the top of every room, with − / +, mute, group volume, and a max volume ceiling you set per room by holding a row.

2

The EQ you were promised

Bass, treble, loudness and Trueplay everywhere. Balance on pairs; surround, height, subwoofer and crossover exactly where the hardware exists — and nowhere it doesn't.

3

Presets that remember everything

Three TV and three music presets per room. Hold to snapshot EQ — plus volume, Night Sound, Speech Boost and Loudness if you tick them. Tap to restore. Siri knows each by name.

4

Build the home theater yourself

Separate front speakers, surrounds, and up to two Subs bonded from inside the app, with a live badge that confirms what the bar is actually decoding.

5

Controls without opening the app

A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, a Home Screen widget with presets and volume, and Siri phrases that work the moment it's installed.

6

Nothing leaves your house

No sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no third-party code. The app talks to your speakers over local Wi-Fi and that is the whole story. Read what it sends.

Every room gets the same foundation. The hardware decides the rest.

Volume, mute, bass, treble, loudness, presets, Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls and the Music sheet appear in every room. Extra controls unlock by room type — and stay hidden where they'd be noise.
