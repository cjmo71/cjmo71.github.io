Crossover

Somos InControl · Crossover speaker check

You have a Sonos Amp, and we recently changed how Somos handles the crossover on it. We want to know whether it works for you — a “yes, it works” counts as much as a “no”, because it tells us where to stop looking.

Nobody on the Somos team owns every Sonos model. Some of these faults we simply cannot reproduce, and guessing has not got us anywhere. A few taps below tells us more than a week of it.

The four yes/no questions are required — they are what settles it. The rest can be skipped, and the app itself sent us nothing — it only opened this page.

1. Do you have a Sonos Amp?

If No, you won't see a crossover control in Somos, and that's correct — there is nothing to send us, so you can stop here.

2. Does the crossover control appear in Somos?
3. Does changing the crossover in Somos actually change the sound?
4. Does crossover work in the OFFICIAL SONOS APP?

Wrong page? Speech Boost has its own questions, and so does a speaker or system that doesn't show up.

Something actually broken instead? Use the support form so it gets triaged as a bug.

Only the yes/no questions are required. Everything else is optional, including your email address — leave it blank and we simply have no way to write back. Your answers go straight to the developer, and the app sent nothing itself; it only opened this page. See our Privacy Policy.
