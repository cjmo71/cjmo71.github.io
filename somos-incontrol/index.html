Version 1.1 (36.0.9) · Public beta on TestFlight

A fast, focused remote for your Sonos® speakers.

Somos InControl gives you one page per room — instant volume, real EQ, sound presets, home theater setup and music — over your local Wi-Fi, with no account, and no cloud for control. It's built for people who want to adjust the sound and get back to whatever they were doing.

Download on TestFlight Read the documentation

Free during the beta · iPhone and iPad · iOS 16.2 or later · Independent, not affiliated with Sonos, Inc.

  1. Install from TestFlightYou'll need Apple's free TestFlight app first. Then tap the link above and install Somos InControl.
  2. Tap Get startedOne screen each for Local Network and Bluetooth, then Find My Speakers. Allow Local Network — it's the only way the app can see your Sonos® speakers. Why it's needed.
  3. Pick a roomThe app scans your network and opens your first room. Everything applies instantly, and a short tour shows you around.

A tour of the app

Real screenshots from the current beta in Demo Mode and dark mode, with what each screen does. Every item links to its page in the guide.

The room page

Each room gets one page. Everything you touch applies to the speaker as you touch it, and the page follows the app's light or dark appearance — this is dark mode, on the Demo Mode room the app lets you explore before it has found your speakers.

  • A large volume slider pinned at the top, with − / + and mute
  • Three TV presets on a soundbar room: hold to save, tap to load — the loaded one is lit
  • Bass, treble and, on this room, a Subwoofer level and on/off switch
  • Your four favorite rooms in the bottom bar — hold a tab to reorder them — with saved groups on its second page
Anatomy of a room page

The room map

Below the sound switches sits a drawing of the room: the soundbar in the centre and every speaker bonded to it, in the place it plays from. Here an Arc with a separate front left and front right.

  • Loudness, Night Sound, Speech Boost, Trueplay, Status Light and Touch Controls, each a single switch
  • Hold the map to open Home Theater Setup
The room diagram

Home Theater Setup

Press and hold the room map on a soundbar's page to open this sheet. Assign speakers to Front Left, Front Right, Surround Left, Surround Right, Subwoofer and Subwoofer 2 — including separate front speakers, which the official app doesn't offer.

  • Up to two Subs, including two older-generation Subs
  • Each assigned speaker has a bulb to flash its light and an × to remove it; the tone switch plays a sound on the speaker being identified
  • Stereo pairs can be created and separated the same way
Speaker setup guide

The Music page

Tap Music in the source row to open it. Your music services sit in a strip under the volume — tap one to browse it — and the player card shows what the speaker is playing. The speaker streams straight from the service; your phone is only the remote.

  • Favorites, then one pill per linked service
  • Album art, title and artist, a scrubber, and previous, play/pause, next, shuffle and repeat
  • Three music presets, separate from the room page's TV presets
  • A TV switch to send a soundbar straight back to the TV input
Music guide

Presets, EQ and the queue

Scroll the Music page and the rest follows: the music presets, a collapsible EQ & Sound card, and the queue with a cover for every song and the playing one marked.

  • Three music presets: hold to save, tap to load
  • EQ & Sound opens to bass, treble, sub, Music Level, Loudness and Full / Ambient
  • The queue, numbered, with Clear at its head
Sound presets

The menu

Everything that isn't a sound control lives here, top-left of every room.

  • Your rooms — tap to switch, hold to reorder
  • System settings opens in place: Live Activity puts volume, mute, your three sound presets and music transport on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; Haptics; Appearance
  • The System heading: the S1 / S2 switcher, for households with both
  • Further down, Help & Support: Settings Somos needs, Getting Started, the Feature Reference, and the rows that reach the developer directly
The menu, section by section

On iPad

The same app, with the room to breathe: on an iPad Pro 11-inch the whole room page fits on one screen. Demo Mode, dark mode, from the current beta.

The room page

Volume, presets, every slider, every switch and the room map, without scrolling. The presets spread into three wide cards and the switches into two columns.

  • Volume, Bass, Treble and Subwoofer sliders with − / + at both ends
  • Sub phase, Normal or Reversed, set by Trueplay while Trueplay is on
  • The room map at the bottom — hold it for Home Theater Setup
Anatomy of a room page

Home Theater Setup

The sheet floats in the middle of the page instead of covering it, so the room stays visible behind it while you assign speakers.

  • The soundbar as coordinator, then a card per position
  • Bulb to flash a speaker's light, × to remove it, Assign speaker… to fill an empty slot
Speaker setup guide

Picking a speaker

Tap Assign speaker… and every speaker in the house that could take the slot is listed with its room. Tap the bulb to flash the one you mean before you commit.

  • Cancel leaves the slot empty
Speaker setup guide

The Music page

The services strip, the player, the music presets and the queue on one screen, each song in the queue with its cover.

  • Favorites and one pill per linked service — Apple Music, Spotify and TuneIn here — then Add
  • The current song is marked in the queue and its cover fills the player
  • Party Mix, Low Bass and Chill: music presets, separate from the TV ones
Music guide

EQ & Sound

Open the card and the music EQ unfolds under the presets: the same bass and treble as the room page, plus the controls that only matter for music.

  • Bass, Treble and Sub level
  • Music Level for the surrounds, and Full ⇄ Ambient for how much of the music they carry
  • Loudness as a switch
EQ and Trueplay

The menu

On the iPad the menu slides over the left third of the page, so the room stays in view. The whole list fits without scrolling.

  • Rooms and System settings at the top
  • Help & Support: the in-app guides, the Apple Watch guide, Support & Feedback and Suggest a Feature
  • Submit diagnostics, and Privacy, Trademark & About
The menu, section by section

The widgets

The Home Screen widgets, shown here at iPad size on a plain background: the medium widget carries two rooms' presets, the large one adds a volume row for a room. Each button loads that preset.

  • Row one: a room's three TV presets; row two: another room's three music presets
  • The loaded preset is lit green
  • Large only: the room name, Vol − and Vol +
Widget and Siri

On Apple Watch

Somos InControl installs a small Apple Watch app alongside the iPhone app. Real screenshots from the Apple Watch Ultra 3 simulator with Demo Mode rooms; the last frame is the app's Smart Stack card drawn on a plain background.

On your wrist: the Apple Watch app

Open Somos on the phone once and your rooms appear on the Watch, in the same order as your bottom bar. Tap a room to see what is playing — the TV input and its audio format, or the song and its cover — then turn the Digital Crown for volume; the Watch talks to the speaker directly over your home Wi-Fi.

  • Rooms come from the phone; the Watch keeps them, even when the phone is out of reach
  • Play / pause and next on a music room, with the cover, title and artist; a TV room shows the format instead — Dolby Atmos (MAT) here
  • The Crown is the volume dial — the number on screen follows it — with Mute beside it
  • The room's three presets from the wrist: TV presets on a TV room, music presets on a music room, the loaded one lit — Party Mix on the Bedroom
  • A Favourites button starts a Sonos® favorite on a music room
  • With Live Activity on, the phone's card also sits in the Watch's Smart Stack — room, what is playing and the volume at a glance, as in the last frame; tap it and the Watch app opens on that room

What's coming — and what just shipped

Music services — sign in to Amazon Music, Pandora, Spotify, iHeartRadio and Bandcamp right from the Music sheet, plus Apple Music (linked in the Sonos app) — are live in the beta now. Your own music server is next.

What works with what

Somos InControl works with Sonos® speakers that support local control — S1 and S2 systems, and more than one system at a time. If a speaker shows in the official app on the same Wi-Fi, it shows here. Every room gets volume, mute, bass, treble, loudness, presets, Trueplay, Status Light, Touch Controls and the Music sheet; the room's hardware adds the rest.

Room typeAdditional controls
Soundbar roomTV / Music source, audio-format badge, Night Sound, Speech Boost (a five-step slider on the Arc Ultra), height on height-capable bars, surround level and on/off, Group Audio Delay, Home Theater Setup
Stereo pairBalance slider, separate-pair option; a music room — its presets and EQ are on the Music sheet
Any room with a bonded SubSubwoofer level and on/off; Sub phase (Normal / Reversed, set by Trueplay while Trueplay is on); crossover (50–110 Hz) wherever the speaker reports one. A Sub never turns a music room into a TV room
Portable (Move, Roam)Battery level in the status line
