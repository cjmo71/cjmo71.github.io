Max volume limit

Somos InControl · Two questions, two taps

Max volume was the per-room ceiling in Somos — hold the volume bar to set a level the room would not go past. It has been removed. We want to know whether you used it, and whether you want it back.

Two taps. Nothing is sent from the app — it only opened this page, which counts the visit and records the page address and nothing about you — and the count below is tallied from the answers and refreshed a few times a day.
