Music services

Somos InControl · One question, your words

Which music services do you use in the official Sonos app? Up to three, in your own words — Apple Music, Spotify, Plex, a radio app, whatever you actually play. The most-named ones are the ones Somos adds next.

No name, no email. Three boxes, one tap. Nothing about you is recorded — only the names you type.
