Speaker Check

Somos InControl · Your speaker refused a setting

Somos tried to change a setting, read it back, and saw that your speaker had not taken it. That is why you are here — the app told you rather than pretending it worked.

Nobody on the Somos team owns every Sonos model. Some of these faults we simply cannot reproduce, and guessing has not got us anywhere. A handful of taps below tells us more than a week of it.

Nothing is required, and the app itself sent us nothing — it only opened this page. Jump to Speech Boost or Crossover.

Speech Boost

Also called dialogue enhancement. Six questions, five of them taps.

1. Which speaker refused it?
2. Right now, can you change Speech Boost for that speaker in the official Sonos app?

This is the one that matters most. Please try it before answering.

3. If it works in the Sonos app — is it a switch, or does it have levels?
4. Was the TV playing through that speaker when Somos refused it?
5. Is that speaker part of a home theatre set-up?

Crossover

The point where bass hands over to your Sub. Five questions.

2. Can you change the crossover for that room in the official Sonos app?
4. Is there a Sub in that room?

Something actually broken instead? Use the support form so it gets triaged as a bug.

Your answers are delivered directly to the developer. The app sent nothing — it only opened this page. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle contact data.
