Speakers Not Found

Somos InControl · Your speaker refused this setting

Somos tried to change that speaker, read it back, and saw that your speaker had not taken it. That is why you are here — the app told you rather than pretending it worked.

Nobody on the Somos team owns every Sonos model. Some of these faults we simply cannot reproduce, and guessing has not got us anywhere. A few taps below tells us more than a week of it.

Every question can be skipped, and the app itself sent us nothing — it only opened this page.

1. Which speaker didn't show up?
2. Right now, does the official Sonos app find that speaker?

This is the one that matters most. Please check before answering.

3. How many separate Sonos systems do you have?

Two systems in one home is the case we are chasing hardest.

4. Are your phone and your speakers on the same Wi-Fi?
5. In the Sonos app, is UPnP turned on?

Sonos app → Settings → System. Somos needs it to find your speakers.

6. Which device are you using, and which OS?

Fill in the one you were using. Somos runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

7. What Sonos software version does the Sonos app show?
8. Which version of Somos are you on?
