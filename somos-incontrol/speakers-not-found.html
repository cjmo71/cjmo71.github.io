Speakers Not Found

Somos InControl · Speakers not found check

Somos looked for your speakers and did not find all of them. That is why you are here — the app told you rather than pretending everything was there.

Nobody on the Somos team owns every Sonos model or every home network. Some of these faults we simply cannot reproduce, and guessing has not got us anywhere. A few taps below tells us more than a week of it.

Every question can be skipped, and the app itself sent us nothing — it only opened this page.

1. Does Somos find all your speakers?

If Yes, skip to the last section.

2. Does the OFFICIAL SONOS APP find all your speakers?
3. How many Sonos systems do you have?
4. Do you have a Sonos system at a different address?
5. Are your phone and your speakers on the same Wi-Fi?
6. Which device are you using, and which OS version?

Somos runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

7. Which version of Somos are you on?

Somos → menu → the version is at the bottom.

Wrong setting? If it was a setting that got refused, that has its own page — answer the Speech Boost questions instead.

Something actually broken instead? Use the support form so it gets triaged as a bug.

Every question here is optional, including your email address — leave it blank and we simply have no way to write back. Your answers go straight to the developer, and the app sent nothing itself; it only opened this page. See our Privacy Policy.
