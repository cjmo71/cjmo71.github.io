The Somos InControl Roadmap built by our users.

Somos is built in the open, shaped directly by what its users ask for. There is no formal roadmap — instead, we follow the direction of our users. Your suggestions are important, so please add yours to our list in the form below.

  • ✅ Faster, more reliable surround & fronts setup — build 31 · July 20

In the current beta — build 35.7.3, being tested now

Finished and in testers’ hands, not yet in the App Store version. Ask for a beta invite if you want it early.

  • 🧪 Split S1 + S2 systems: your S2 rooms no longer go missing
  • 🧪 Music and TV are a real toggle instead of looking disabled
  • 🧪 Portable speakers (Move, Move 2, Roam) show their battery level

Still to come

  • ◻️ Crossover control still goes missing for some systems — not solved; if this is you, the support form helps us more than anything
  • ◻️ Distance settings for surrounds
  • ◻️ Sleep timer — stop the music after a set time — planned, going in the advanced menu
  • ◻️ Separate TV and music speaker profiles — presets already do this by hand; switching them on their own when the TV goes off is what is still missing
  • ◻️ Turn Speech Enhancement off by itself when the soundbar goes from TV to music
  • ◻️ Hide the TV settings on a speaker that never has a TV on it
  • ◻️ Give dedicated front speakers the same adjustment range the rears have
  • ◻️ Reorder the speakers on the bottom bar, and hide the ones you don’t use
  • ◻️ A preset that applies to a whole group, not one room at a time
  • ◻️ One-tap scenes and macros
  • ◻️ Permanent or scheduled groups — group every room at a set time, play to all of them at another
  • ◻️ Night Mode that turns itself off, and a scheduled quiet mode
  • ◻️ Lock the volume so it does not jump when the TV changes source
  • ◻️ Send the TV speakers back to the group they came from when the TV goes off
  • ◻️ Show which speaker is running a group
  • ◻️ Make the group volume slider easy to find on any room in the group
  • ◻️ A source indicator while something is playing — Dolby Atmos and the rest
  • ◻️ Play from your own library, and from streaming services, inside Somos — being built now, part of the new music page
  • ◻️ Albums as a grid with artwork instead of a list
  • ◻️ An adjustable sub crossover rather than a fixed one
  • ◻️ Turn on the Era 300’s own front drivers while it is being used as a surround
  • ◻️ Use two Move 2 portables as surrounds with a soundbar
  • ◻️ Add a stereo pair to a home theatre, or add a soundbar to an existing pair
  • ◻️ A preset that moves a front speaker back to being a surround
  • ◻️ In-app test tones, with the speaker to test picked by you
  • ◻️ An external physical volume knob
  • ◻️ An Android version — asked for most often
  • ◻️ A Mac version
  • ◻️ Apple TV support

Asked for, but we can’t

Requested more than once. Sonos does not expose these, so no app can do them.

  • ❌ A full graphic EQ — bass and treble are the only tone controls Sonos exposes
  • ❌ Side-channel speakers, alongside fronts and rears
