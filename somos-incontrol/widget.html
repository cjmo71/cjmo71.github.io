Widget

Somos InControl · How is the Home Screen widget behaving?

The Somos Home Screen widget puts your presets and volume one tap away. It works on a lot of setups and not on some — and a widget is a hard thing for us to see from the outside, especially as Apple keeps changing how they behave from one iOS version to the next. A few taps below tells us exactly what yours is doing.

This is about the Home Screen widget. Every question can be skipped, and the app itself sent us nothing — it only opened this page.

1. Does the Somos widget appear when you add it to your Home Screen?
2. When adding it, can you pick which room it controls?
3. Did you name your presets before adding the widget?

This sets up the next question — a preset with no name of its own shows as "Preset 1/2/3", and that is expected.

4. If you named them — do the preset buttons show your real preset names?
5. Does the preset that's currently playing show up GREEN?
6. When you tap a preset, does it play without the widget closing or freezing?
7. Do the Vol + and Vol − buttons change the volume?
8. Does the Mute button mute the room?
9. Which widget size are you using?
10. Which device are you using, and which iOS version?

The iOS version matters most here — Apple changes how widgets behave with almost every release.

11. Which version of Somos are you on?

Something actually broken instead? Use the support form so it gets triaged as a bug.
